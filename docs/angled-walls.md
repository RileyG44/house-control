## 1. Plan format (backward compatible; every existing plan must load and look the same)

### Rooms: polygons (P0)
- A room has either `rect: [x, z, w, d]` (unchanged) or `poly: [[x, z], ...]`: a simple polygon,
  at least 3 points, any angles, either winding. `normalize()` gives every room `points` (a rect
  becomes its 4 corners), `area`, and keeps `x, z, w, d` as the bounding box so old code paths that
  only need a box keep working. Reject self-intersecting or degenerate polygons with a clear
  `ConfigError`.
- Nesting stays as it is: a room drawn inside another (bathroom inside the studio, shower inside the
  bathroom) is fine; whether a wall is interior or exterior is still decided by whether there is a
  room on both sides of it, using point-in-polygon. Floors of nested rooms layer by area.

### Walls (P0)
- `wallSegments(config)` works on polygon edges at any angle: edges are grouped by their infinite
  line (canonical direction + offset, 1 mm tolerance), split at every edge end and opening end,
  classified and merged exactly as today. Each segment carries both end points, its unit direction
  and outward normal, `kind`, `opening`, free-end flags. Axis-aligned segments may keep
  `axis`/`c`/`a`/`b` for convenience, but no consumer may assume them.
- Corners: where two non-collinear walls meet at an angle, the joint must be closed in 3D and in
  the 2D plan (mitre the boxes or drop a small column of wall thickness at the joint; no gaps, no
  spikes poking through the other side).

### Openings (P0)
- `from`/`to` may lie on any wall line, including angled ones (both points within 1 cm of the line).
  The "must be axis-aligned" check goes.
- Doors: optional `hinge: "from" | "to"` (default `"from"`) and `swing: "<room id>"`, the room the
  leaf opens into (default: the smaller of the two rooms, today's behaviour). On an exterior wall
  with no room on one side, `swing: "out"` is allowed.
- `open`: optional `curtain: true` (a shower curtain or closet curtain: rod + gathered cloth).

### Furniture (P0)
New types, each with a 3D builder in `three/furniture.js` (same style as the rest: rounded boxes,
matte materials, facing +z, standing on y = 0), a `footprint`, a 2D glyph in `edit/glyphs.js`, a
name/icon in the editor catalog, sensible defaults, and entries in `RESIZABLE`/`COLORABLE` where
they apply:

| type | what | options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `counter` | run of base cabinets with countertop, no hob | `width`, `depth` (0.62), `sink`: `none` / `single` / `double`, `sinkAt` (offset of the sink centre from the middle, m), `top` colour |
| `stove` | freestanding range: white body, 4 coil burners, oven door + handle, back panel with knobs | `width` (0.76) |
| `microwave` | countertop microwave, black, door window + keypad | `y` (base height, default 0.9) |
| `tvCabinet` | wood cabinet (drawers + door) with dark granite top and a flat TV on a stand on it | `width` (0.95), `tv` (screen width, default width − 0.05) |
| `beanBag` | soft squashed blob (deformed sphere, not a cylinder) | `size` ([w, d]), `color` |
| `waterHeater` | white cylinder tank with pipes on top | `diameter` (0.55), `height` (1.5) |
| `chair` | single dining chair, wood frame, dark seat | `color` |

## 2. 3D scene (P0)
- `three/house.js`: floors from polygons (ShapeGeometry or equivalent, same materials/UVs feel),
  walls as boxes rotated to their segment, windows/doors/open pieces work on angled walls, the
  camera-side cut-away uses the segment normal for any angle, interior wall cut height and the
  invisible full-height shadow casters as today.
- Door leaves (P1): draw a thin leaf on hinge/swing, open about 30°, so the swing direction reads in
  3D. `curtain: true` openings get a rod and a gathered translucent curtain at one end.
- Everything else that reads rooms (`world.js` hit testing, room filter highlight, lights per room,
  `widgets.js`, `agent/*`, `island/*`) must use `points`/point-in-polygon instead of rect math
  where the shape matters.

## 3. Editor (P0 unless marked)
- `edit/model.js` and `edit/plan.js`: draw polygon rooms (fill, outline, label at a point inside the
  polygon), walls from the new `wallSegments`, windows and doors on angled walls, door arcs that
  follow `hinge`/`swing`. Hit testing, `roomAt`, `contains`, `area`, `wallLines`, `nearestWall`,
  `placeOnWall`, `openingSpan`/`setOpeningSpan`, `openingRooms`, `addOpening`, `contentsOf`,
  `riders`, `roomDeletion`, `duplicate` all handle polygon rooms and angled walls. Dragging an
  opening slides it along its (possibly angled) wall.
- Moving a polygon room translates its points (and its riders, like today).
- Room inspector: polygon rooms show area and corner count instead of the width/depth steppers.
- **Cut corner** (P0): on a selected rectangular or polygon room, a "Cut corner" action chamfers the
  chosen corner by a length (stepper, default 0.6 m), turning a rect into a `poly`. This is how a
  user makes an angled wall like the bathroom's.
- Corner handles (P1): a polygon room shows draggable corner handles; dragging snaps to the grid, to
  other rooms' corners and edges, and to 45° from the neighbouring corner.
- Opening inspector: for doors, "Hinge" (left/right) and "Opens into" (the two rooms, or Outside)
  segmented controls; for open passages, a "Curtain" toggle.
- The Add catalog lists the new furniture (grouped with the kitchen and bathroom pieces where the
  catalog groups things), with glyph previews like the others.
- AI agent tools (`agent/tools.js`, `plan-edit.js`) (P1): accept `poly` rooms and the new types, and
  the system prompt stops saying rooms must be rectangles.
